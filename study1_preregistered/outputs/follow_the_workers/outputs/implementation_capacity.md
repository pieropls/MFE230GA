Capacity is unverified. An implementation assessment still needs intended capital and position sizes, executable spreads/depth/volume, holdings and methodology histories, short locates/borrow/recall terms, financing and market-impact/participation assumptions. Fund existence or asset size alone does not establish capacity. French industry portfolios are research series, not directly tradable instruments. The current capacity record is therefore explicitly incomplete; the model cannot pass G5 on available evidence. This limitation is recorded before research performance is examined.
