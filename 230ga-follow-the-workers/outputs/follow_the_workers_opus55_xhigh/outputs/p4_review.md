# Opus P4 audit dispositions

Research gives no compelling implementation evidence. Preserve mechanical A4 selection and all registered final diagnostics; neither the auditor nor engineer changes features, signs, thresholds or the research/test boundary.

The ten suggestions are audit claims, not instructions to change the study. All numerical checks used saved research ledgers; no test returns were read and no backtest was repeated.

All research gross means are negative, but the quoted -0.40 to -0.55 Sharpe range describes net, not both gross and net; gross Sharpes are approximately -0.25 to -0.38.

## 1. Limited power and noisy rankings

**Retain registered horizons diagnostic.** Retain registered HAC IC estimates and stationary-bootstrap uncertainty for the declared paired IC/return comparisons. The quoted Sharpe precision is an IID approximation, not a formal power analysis. Do not add new Sharpe-difference test families or a confidence-bound implementation gate.

## 2. Common construction or hedge effects

**Retain registered placebo diagnostic.** Retain the prescribed causal circular-shift placebo, factor attribution and accounting checks. A fit across five related strategy summaries cannot establish a common hedge drag. Do not add 500 cross-sectional shuffles, an own-IC regression veto or a new percentile threshold.

## 3. Alleged inconsistent benchmark

**Reject proposed change.** Unsupported: active return belongs to a separate long-only portfolio, whereas net is hedged long-short excess return. Their subtraction does not recover a benchmark. data.py uses the same equal-weight group excess-return benchmark and all five ledgers share the same dates/universe; saved active-return metrics reconcile. No benchmark or return-series change is warranted.

## 4. Cost and implementation optimism

**Retain registered cost_25 diagnostic.** Retain registered 0/10/25bp industry costs, borrow/financing sensitivity, break-even analysis and capacity gate. All research gross means are negative, so costs are not the sole source of losses. Actual capacity and market impact remain unverified. Do not add a new twice-estimated-cost threshold or fabricate liquidity estimates.

## 5. Trial accounting and near-clone strategies

**Reject proposed change.** The 41 count is nominal: 36 first-repetition candidate slots plus five strategies, including one invalid candidate. All slots are accounted for; 35 valid candidate portfolios and five strategy portfolios are saved. A2 was excluded by pre-existing source-admission failures, not performance. Preserve the undefined full-family reality check and DSR caveat; no new SPA/Romano-Wolf family, effective-trial tuning or DSR gate.

## 6. Sign instability and post-hoc inversion

**Retain registered halves diagnostic.** Use the registered half-period IC sign checks. No signal inversion, extra candidates or model reselection is authorized by a negative research result. Do not add inverted trials or a DSR0.95 cutoff. The claimed implication that an inverse cannot invert portfolio profit is not established by the five-point fit.

## 7. Regime concentration and favorable windows

**Retain registered tightness diagnostic.** Use registered tightness, pandemic, half-period, last-window and profit-concentration diagnostics. Aggregate drawdown minus an approximate log loss cannot establish the presence or absence of a profitable stretch, especially when funded wealth includes the risk-free return. Do not choose start dates from outcomes or add a three-year recurrence threshold.

## 8. Outliers and low hit rate

**Reject proposed change.** Do not introduce trimming, a Wilcoxon/sign-test trading gate or a new median-return threshold. The quoted sign-test inference would require dependence assumptions; a hit rate below one half does not itself imply a negative expected payoff. Preserve the existing profit-concentration gate and full untrimmed return histories.

## 9. Timing, horizon and universe alignment

**Retain registered variant_gap diagnostic.** P1 release/cutoff checks, training-label assertions and synthetic timing tests passed. Retain registered ASOF/FIRST/REVISED/FIXEDLAG, horizon and holding-period diagnostics. Full-universe rank IC need not have the same sign as a tail-selected, overlapping, hedged portfolio. No new lead/lag grid or lag selection follows this audit; point-in-time tradability of French baskets remains a disclosed limitation.

## 10. Alleged funded-account mismatch

**Reject proposed change.** Saved-ledger reconstruction passed for all five strategies: net=gross-cost-financing, total_net=net+risk_free, funded drawdown compounds total_net, and Sharpe uses net excess returns. Weights share the funded-capital basis and the gross cap reconciles. This intentional distinction is not an accounting bug. Do not invent an unregistered drawdown-budget veto.
