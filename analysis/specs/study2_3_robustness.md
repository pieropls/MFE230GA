# Study 2.3: additional robustness (frozen before running)

**Label: POST-HOC.** Written on 4 Oct 2026, after the test period of Study 1 had been opened and after Studies 2.1 (variants W, SC, W+MOM, HQ12) and 2.2 (HQ12 battery) had been run and read. Nothing here can change the confirmatory verdict of Study 1 (A3, "Do not implement", test Sharpe −0.58), and no item is allowed to produce a new headline strategy. Every item is reported whatever its result. This file is SHA-256-hashed (`study2_3_robustness.sha256`) and committed before any item below is run; the items are then run once.

Names: W, SC, W+MOM and HQ12 are the variants frozen as V2a, V2b, V2c and V2d in `study2_1_variants.md`; A0 (fixed rule) and A3 (agent features, independent; primary) are Study 1 strategies. Windows are the ones of `study2_1_variants.md`: research May 2004–Sep 2014, test Nov 2014–Aug 2026, full May 2004–Aug 2026, post-2010, last 18 months.

## 0. Tools fixed in advance

- **Backtest engine.** `analysis/data.py::backtest_variant` re-implements Study 1's `data.backtest` (tranches, 60-month beta hedge, Ledoit–Wolf covariance, 10% volatility target, gross cap, 10 bp + 2 bp costs) with two extra arguments: the gross cap (2, 3 or none) and the tranche weighting (top 3 / bottom 3, or rank-weighted). It also records, each month, the ex-ante annualised volatility of the unit book (tranche average plus hedge, before scaling) and the gross exposure needed to reach the 10% target. Validation: with the default arguments it must reproduce the net returns of Study 1's own `backtest` to 1e-10 for the saved A3 scores and for HQ12; if it does not, items R2, R3 and the Study 1 cap and rank-weighted diagnostics are marked "not run: engine validation failed".
- **Inference.** Rank IC, Newey–West errors (6 lags for one-month statistics, h+5 for an h-month target), block bootstrap (12-month blocks, 5,000 draws, seed 230, 90% percentile interval) and the factor regression are the functions of `analysis/stats.py`, unchanged.
- **Looks.** Each new backtest is one more look. Count before running: R1 = 24 (four variants at 10 bp; HQ12 at 0, 25 and 50 bp; two components; two holding periods; thirteen drop-one runs), R2 = 4, R3 = 2. Total 30, so deflated Sharpe ratios in Study 2.3 use **N = 132 + 30 = 162**. Earlier tables keep the count that applied when they were run (112 for Study 2.1, 132 for Study 2.2).

## 2. Study 2.3 items

**R1. As-known rerun of W, SC, W+MOM and HQ12.** Data: Alex Roesler's package (`analysis/data/alex_package/`), Study 1 outputs `panel_asof.parquet` (every series as known at each month-end decision date) and `features_asof.parquet`.
- *Validation first.* (i) Rebuild the raw features F1–F5 from `panel_asof.parquet` with Study 1's transforms (reference months m_J and m_C = latest months with at least 11 groups observed; 3-month averages; 12-month differences, log differences for hours) and compare with `raw_value` in `features_asof.parquet`: maximum absolute difference below 1e-9. (ii) Form A0 = (z(F1) + z(F2) − z(F4) + z(F5))/4 from the standardised values in `features_asof.parquet` and compare with the saved `ASOF_A0_scores.csv`: maximum absolute difference below 1e-9 in the test window. If either check fails, R1 is marked "not run: validation failed". (The prompt for this run named `levels_asof.parquet`; that file holds only the reference-month level per decision, which is not enough to rebuild a 12-month change of 3-month averages as known, so the full as-known panel is used instead.)
- *Scores.* W raw = log of the 3-month average of average hourly earnings at m_C minus the same at m_C − 12, as known at each decision, standardised with Study 1's transform; z(F1)…z(F5) are the standardised values of `features_asof.parquet`. W = cross-sectional rank of z(W); SC = (1/6) Σ sign_k z_k with signs re-learned on the as-known research months; W+MOM = average of the ranks of z(W) and of industry momentum 12−1; HQ12 = −(z(F2) + z(F3))/2 held 12 months. One continuous run May 2004–Aug 2026 through Study 1's backtest at 10 bp, exactly as in Study 2.1.
- *Outputs.* For each variant: Sharpe by window, test IC (one month; 12 months for HQ12) with t, test alpha t, next to the fixed-lag numbers of Study 2.1; share of test months in which the three highest-ranked groups, and the three lowest-ranked, are the same as in the fixed-lag version. For HQ12 the Study 2.2 battery as known: components (hires only, quits only), holding 9 and 18 months, test halves, costs 0/25/50 bp and break-even, drop one group (minimum and maximum), block-bootstrap interval (test and full), horizon profile, alpha with 12 lags.
- *Reading, fixed now.* "As-known confirms the fixed-lag reading" if, for HQ12, the test Sharpe and the 12-month test IC keep their sign and the test Sharpe moves by at most 0.15; otherwise "as-known differs", and the as-known number is the one quoted first. Either way the label stays post-hoc.

**R2. Risk-overlay diagnostic.** Test window. Distribution (5th, 50th, 95th percentile) of the ex-ante volatility of the unit book and of the gross exposure needed to reach 10%. A3 (saved as-known scores) and HQ12 (fixed-lag scores of Study 2.1) with the cap at 2 (base), 3 and no cap. Table: Sharpe, realised volatility, mean gross exposure, share of months in which the cap binds.

**R3. Rank-weighted books.** Tranche weights proportional to the demeaned rank of the score across all 13 groups, scaled so that longs sum to +1 and shorts to −1; same tranche averaging (3 months for A3, 12 for HQ12), hedge, volatility target, cap 2 and costs. Test window: Sharpe, transfer coefficient (mean monthly correlation between the group weights held and the demeaned score), monthly turnover, and the implied information ratio IC × √BR × TC (BR = 156 a year for A3, 13 for HQ12), next to the top-3/bottom-3 numbers.

**R4. Agents: arm gap against seed spread.** From `outputs/candidates.csv` and `outputs/selected_features.json` of Study 1 and Study 1b. Per study × arm × seed: valid proposals, best and mean research IC of valid proposals, eligible features (seed 1 only, the only seed evaluated for eligibility), share of proposals with a `regime()` gate. Exact permutation test on the islands-minus-independent difference of seed means (all 20 relabelings of six seeds into two arms; two-sided p = share of relabelings with an absolute difference at least as large as observed; the smallest possible p is 0.10), for best IC and mean IC. One dot plot. Expected message, stated before looking: the arm gap is within the seed spread.

**R5. Tokens per arm.** From the `tokens` field of `outputs/agents/log.jsonl` (candidate calls) in both studies: total and mean tokens per call by arm; Spearman correlation between a proposal's tokens and its research IC. If the field does not hold usage counts, the item is marked "not run: no usage in the log".

**R6. Effective breadth.** Participation ratio (Σλ)²/Σλ² of the eigenvalues of the correlation matrix of (a) the 13 group excess returns, (b) the 13 group relative returns, (c) z(W) across groups, (d) the HQ12 score across groups; full sample.

**R7. Capacity (gate G5).** Average daily dollar volume over the 252 trading days to 30 Sep 2026 of each proxy ETF in `etf_proxies.csv` and of SPY (Yahoo Finance through `yfinance`). Mean monthly one-way turnover per group of A3 and HQ12 in the test window. AUM at which the monthly trade in the most constrained ETF reaches 1% and 5% of one day's dollar volume. If the data cannot be downloaded: "not run: no data access".

**R8. Judge fix.** The 25 hand-labelled report–proposal pairs (8 in Study 1, 17 in Study 1b). The original judge prompt, with one change: the judge must return JSON `{"uses_claim": true|false, "quote": "<one sentence copied verbatim from the report, or none>"}`, and a "yes" counts only if the quote appears verbatim in the report (whitespace and case normalised). Original models (`claude-sonnet-5-5` for Study 1 pairs, `claude-opus-5-5` for Study 1b), headless, no tools, one call per pair, at most 5 minutes per call, no repair of a malformed answer (it counts as undefined). Output: agreement with the human labels before and after, and the number of false "yes". Every call is logged.

**R9. Feedback ablation.** Independent arm only, **research data only** (May 2004–Sep 2014; no test return is read). Model `claude-sonnet-5-5`, Study 1's system prompt, grammar, dictionary, blinding, validation and evaluation code, three agents (Temporal, Composition, Interactions), six submissions each. Two conditions: (A) the original feedback, which includes the IC in the low and high tightness terciles; (B) the same feedback without those two fields. Seeds are run in order 1, 2, 3, both conditions per seed, until 2 hours have elapsed; at most 5 minutes per call; a failed call consumes its slot. Outcomes, measured on the proposals themselves: share of valid proposals that use `regime()`, and share of valid proposals active (non-missing for at least 11 groups) in fewer than 50% of research months. Whatever finished is reported, with the number of proposals per condition. With one to three seeds this is a small sample; no significance claim is made.
